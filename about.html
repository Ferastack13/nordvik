About

Furniture rooted in Nordic life

Since 1978, Nordvik has designed and crafted furniture for homes that value calm, honesty, and lasting comfort.

Heritage

From a small Copenhagen atelier

Nordvik began when two designers opened a workshop near the harbour — drawn to oak, wool, and the idea that furniture should serve life without demanding attention. Decades later, that same ethos guides every collection.

We work with makers across Denmark, Sweden, and Norway. Each partnership is built on shared standards: responsible materials, repairable construction, and silhouettes that remain relevant long after trends pass.

Honesty

Materials are left close to their nature. Grain, weave, and texture are celebrated — never disguised by excess finish or ornament.

Proportion

We design for architecture as much as for the body. Clear lines, balanced volumes, and generous but careful scale.

Longevity

A Nordvik piece is meant to be lived with for decades — refinished, re-covered, and passed on with its story intact.

Milestones

Our journey

1978

Founded in Copenhagen

The first Nordvik workshop opens, producing dining chairs and small oak tables for local homes and architects.

1994

Stockholm showroom

Our first dedicated showroom invites people to experience furniture in full interior compositions.

2008

Material charter

We formalise our commitments to certified woods, natural textiles, and low-impact finishes.

2019

Haven Collection

A landmark living collection that redefined modular softness for contemporary Scandinavian homes.

Today

Homes across the world

From Oslo apartments to international residences — always with the same quiet ambition: furniture that feels like home.

Sustainability

Responsibility woven into process

We measure success by how long our furniture remains useful — and by how carefully we treat the landscapes and communities that make it possible.

Sourcing

FSC-certified timber, OEKO-TEX fabrics, and suppliers audited for environmental and social standards.

Repair

Replaceable covers, spare parts, and refinishing guidance so pieces can evolve with their owners.

Future

Expanding take-back and refurbishment programmes across our Nordic showrooms by 2028.
