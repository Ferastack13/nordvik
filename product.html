Craftsmanship

Built to settle into the room

The Fjord frame is kiln-dried hardwood with reinforced joints. Seat cushions use layered high-resilience foam and feather wrap for a sit that softens without collapsing. Oak legs are hand-oiled to reveal natural grain.

Designer note

“We reduced the visual weight of the sofa so it could live easily with architecture — low arms, calm lines, and fabric that invites touch.”

Care

Vacuum regularly with a soft brush. Spot clean with mild soap. Oak legs may be refreshed with natural oil annually.
